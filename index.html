In progress · iOS · Solo product

Batu (working name)

An emergency storage buffer for iPhone. Batu sets space aside in advance and gives it back in one tap when your phone is full at the worst moment.

  • Positioning: the closest competitor sells the same mechanism as a developer testing tool; Batu reframes it for everyday users who need space right now.
  • Trade-off: free reserves expire after ~30 days instead of 7, so users feel the payoff before anything is taken away; Pro removes the expiry.
  • Built with Claude Code; chunk-based reserves so resizing never leaves the user without a buffer.

Also in progress: a case study on the enterprise billing integration at Telkomsel.
